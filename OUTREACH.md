# Outreach — Getting Your First Client

## Who to target first (narrow = faster yes)

Pick **one** tight niche where you can name the pain and reach them in bulk:
- **Independent real-estate agents** on 99acres / MagicBricks / Housing.com listings —
  their phone number is public on every listing.
- **Small property dealers** in one city/area (your prompts are Hindi/Telugu/English ready —
  Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bangalore all fit).
- Find 30–50 numbers. DM/call all of them. Expect ~10% to try, ~1–2 to convert. That's
  your first client.

## The rule

Lead with the **live demo**, not the explanation. The whole goal of every message is
*"talk to Riya for 60 seconds."* Once they hear it, you've half-closed.

---

## Message 1 — WhatsApp / first touch (short)

> Namaste 🙏 Main ek AI receptionist banata hoon jo aapke property calls 24/7 Hinglish
> mein uthati hai, buyer ka budget/location/timeline poochti hai, aur lead turant aapko
> WhatsApp pe bhejti hai — taaki ek bhi missed call se buyer haath se na nikle.
>
> 60 second mein khud sun lijiye 👇 [DEMO LINK]
>
> Pasand aaye toh main aapke number pe **free 7-day pilot** laga deta hoon. 🙂

## Message 2 — if they reply "interesting / how much"

> Bahut simple hai — ₹4,999/mo se start, ek receptionist se kaafi sasta aur 24/7.
> Setup main karta hoon, aapko kuch nahi karna. Free 7-day pilot mein aap dekh lijiye
> kitni leads aati hain, phir decide kijiye. Aaj aapka number set kar doon?

## Message 3 — if no reply (follow-up after 2 days)

> Ek line follow-up 🙂 — kal raat 9 baje agar koi buyer call karta aur aap busy hote,
> toh Riya uthaa leti aur lead aapko mil jaati. Demo abhi bhi live hai: [DEMO LINK].
> 60 second lagega.

---

## Cold-call opener (15 seconds)

> "Namaste sir, main [naam]. Aap property listings karte hain na? Ek quick baat — maine
> ek AI assistant banaya hai jo aapke missed calls uthata hai 24/7 aur buyer ki details
> WhatsApp pe bhej deta hai. Main aapko abhi ek number deta hoon, aap usse call karke
> 30 second baat karke dekhiye — agar pasand aaye toh free trial laga dunga. Theek hai?"

Then send the number/link. Let the product talk.

---

## Email subject lines (if emailing agencies)

- `Aapke missed property calls = lost buyers. 24/7 AI fix, free trial.`
- `AI receptionist for [Agency] — answers every call in Hinglish`
- `60-second demo: an AI that captures every property lead`

---

## Handling the 3 objections you'll hear

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "AI buyers ko pasand nahi aayega" | "Khud suniye — Riya bilkul natural Hinglish bolti hai. 60 sec demo, phir aap batao." |
| "Abhi nahi / sochta hoon" | "Bilkul — isliye free 7-day pilot hai. Risk zero. Main aaj laga doon, leads dekh lijiye, phir decide." |
| "Mera number kaise kaam karega" | "Wahi number rahega, ya naya de deta hoon. Setup main karta hoon, 10 minute mein live." |

---

## Your weekly target

- **50 outreach messages** → ~5 demos → **1 pilot** → **1 paying client.**
- Track replies in a simple sheet. Double down on whatever niche replies most.
