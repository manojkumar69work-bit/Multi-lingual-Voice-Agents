# Riya — Your 24/7 AI Receptionist for Real Estate (Hinglish)

**Never miss a lead again.** Riya answers every call in natural Hinglish, qualifies the
buyer, and drops the lead straight into your sheet/WhatsApp — day, night, weekends.

---

## The problem (open with this)

A real-estate agent who misses a call usually loses the deal — the buyer just calls the
next listing. You can't pick up while you're showing a property, driving, or sleeping.
Hiring a receptionist costs ₹15,000–25,000/month and they work 8 hours, not 24.

## What Riya does

- **Answers instantly, 24/7** — every call, in fluent Hinglish (Hindi + English mix).
- **Qualifies the lead** — asks for budget, location, property type, timeline, contact —
  naturally, like a trained tele-caller.
- **Delivers the lead in real time** — to your **Google Sheet** and **WhatsApp**, the
  moment the call ends.
- **Records & summarises every call** — you get an AI summary + audio playback in your
  own private dashboard.
- **Your brand** — Riya introduces herself with *your* agency name and your script.
