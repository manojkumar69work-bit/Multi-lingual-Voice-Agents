How a call works

From “hello” to a lead in your inbox.

Nothing to install for your callers. They dial your number; the agent does the rest while you're with another customer.

01

Answers

Picks up on the first ring with your greeting, your business name and your agent's name.

Any hour, any number of calls at once
02

Listens

Waits for the caller to finish — a pause for breath or an “achha” doesn't cut them off. Stops talking when they interrupt.

Semantic turn-taking
03

Answers questions

Uses the facts you give it — projects, prices, areas, office hours — and never invents what isn't there.

Your business facts only
04

Hands you the lead

Name, number, what they want, budget and timeline go to WhatsApp, Google Sheets or your CRM — while the call is still going.

Retried until it lands
Languages

Sounds like your city, not a call centre.

Callers mix languages mid-sentence. The agent does too — in the register people actually use on the phone.

Hinglish
“Aapka budget kitna hai? Aur aap kis area mein dekh rahe hain?”
Hindi in everyday Roman script, with English words left as they are — how urban callers speak.
తెలుగు
“మీ budget ఎంత అండి? ఏ area లో చూస్తున్నారు?”
Spoken Telugu with the English loanwords callers already use — not a translation.
English
“What's your budget, and which areas are you considering?”
Indian English voices for callers who prefer it.
